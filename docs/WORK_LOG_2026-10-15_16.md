## Step 2: Four Bug Fixes (October 15)

### 2a. Wrong-company insider data

- **Problem:** when Yahoo had no Canadian insider data, the lookup tried the same
  letters on US markets: TELUS `T` → AT&T, Cameco `CCO` → Clear Channel,
  Methanex `MX` → Magnachip, WELL Health → Welltower, Air Canada `AC` →
  Associated Capital. Insider buying adds a score point and card lines.
- **Fix:**
  - `yf_symbols.yf_ticker_candidates()` no longer offers a bare (US) symbol.
  - `insider_transactions.get_insider_summary()` queries only the resolved
    Canadian listing plus the curated same-issuer US listing (TELUS → `TU`,
    Cameco → `CCJ`); otherwise insider data stays unknown.
- **Verified live:** Yahoo "404" errors fell from 73 (Session 1, old code) to 4
  (Session 2, new code); `.V` fallback lookups fell from 42 to 0.
- **Not changed (noted for later):** the curated map entry `NA` points to an
  unrelated Nasdaq symbol (National Bank is not on the watchlist), and Barrick's
  `GOLD` mapping should be rechecked.

### 2b. Telegram HTML escaping

- **Problem:** stock cards, card footers, catalyst alerts, the news-LLM summary,
  portfolio review reasons and ownership holder names inserted external or model
  text into HTML without escaping. One `&` or `<` made Telegram return HTTP 400;
  the signal was then not logged.
- **Fix:**
  - Values are escaped with `telegram_notify.esc()` / `enrichment._esc()` and
    `html.escape` in those senders.
  - `telegram_notify.send()` retries once as plain text when Telegram reports an
    HTML entity error. The rejected message was not delivered, so this cannot
    duplicate it.

### 2c. DeepSeek final-answer parsing

- **Problem:** triage sometimes returned a draft JSON, reasoning text, DeepSeek's
  end-of-thinking marker, then the real JSON (`logs/llm_debug_triage_20261015_083305.txt`).
  Parsing failed and a retry pass was spent.
- **Fix:** `llm_researcher._strip_fences()` keeps only the text after the last
  end-of-thinking marker (`<｜end▁of▁thinking｜>` or `</think>`).
  - A draft before the marker is never used.
  - Reasoning with nothing after the marker is a logged failure.
  - Two unmarked JSON objects still fail rather than guess.

### 2d. Strike label on option cards

- **Problem:** cards always said "(ATM)". On October 14, CNQ 72 (spot 74.27) and
  SU 104 (spot 107.99) were 3-4% in the money.
- **Fix:** `options_signals._moneyness_label()` shows "ATM" within 1% of spot,
  otherwise the ITM/OTM percentage, for example "3.1% ITM".

## Step 3: Real Clicks For The 40-Minute Check (October 15)

- **Request:** real clicks in the IBKR Paper page every 40 minutes, not just
  address changes.
- **How it works now** (`CanadianResearchBrowser._portfolio_navigation`):
  1. A page probe (`TAB_PROBE`) re-checks the portal domain, Paper banner,
     password/2FA/CAPTCHA text and dialogs.
  2. It finds exactly one Dashboard + Positions tab strip and confirms nothing
     covers the target tab.
  3. OpenClaw `click-coords` sends a real mouse click on **Positions**; the
     helper confirms Positions became active.
  4. After three seconds it clicks **Dashboard** and confirms it.
  5. A click is never repeated; failures record a short safe reason code.
- **Verified:** the probe passed mock-page scenarios under Node on VM 105.
- **First live runs:** stopped safely at `dialog_requires_attention` (Step 4).

## Step 6: IBKR Paper Option Chains (October 15)

- **Check first:** the owner confirmed in the IBKR Option Chain screen that the
  Paper account shows CDE (Montreal) option bid/ask for BCE. A read-only test
  then confirmed IBKR's portal data service returns:
  - the TSE listing separately from the NYSE twin;
  - every monthly and weekly expiry;
  - strikes;
  - multiplier 100;
  - bid/ask/sizes, open interest, IBKR delta and implied volatility.
- **Owner decisions:**
  - Read every expiry, but recommend only 21-45 days (unchanged).
  - Run in Session 2 only (unchanged).
- **New module `ibkr_options.py`.** Inside the verified Paper tab, for every
  CALL/PUT candidate name:
  1. Look up the stock, accepting only the `TSE` or `VENTURE` listing.
  2. Read the months covering 21-45 days and the CDE strikes; keep 4 strikes
     below and 4 above the last close for the needed side.
  3. Read contract details (conid, maturity, multiplier, CAD, CDE) for every
     expiry in the window.
  4. Read quote snapshots: bid, ask, sizes, volume, open interest,
     availability (real-time/delayed/frozen), delta, implied volatility.
- **Safety:**
  - Only GET requests to `secdef/search`, `secdef/strikes`, `secdef/info`,
    `marketdata/snapshot` and `marketdata/unsubscribeall` are possible. A
    blocked-path guard is inside the page script.
  - No clicks, typing, order, account or portfolio requests.
  - Runs under the shared browser lock.
- **Shared quality gates (unchanged), applied to IBKR rows too:**
  - 21-45 DTE.
  - Quote at most 30 minutes old.
  - Bid/ask spread at most 25%.
  - Open interest at least 100 or volume at least 10.
- **IBKR-specific checks:**
  - Rows must be real-time or delayed (`6509` R/D); frozen, not-subscribed and
    prior-close values are rejected.
  - A delayed quote's time is IBKR's update time minus 15 minutes.
- **Wiring:**
  - `research_pipeline.verify_shortlist()` runs IBKR first (own 300-second
    budget), then the unchanged MX/review stage for names IBKR did not cover
    (logged out, lookup failure, rate limit).
  - `session_runner.last_closes()` supplies prices for strike selection.
- **Cards:**
  - "Source: IBKR Paper".
  - Contract premium for 100 shares.
  - IBKR delta/IV (supplied by IBKR, not modelled).
- **Verified:**
  - Offline tests.
  - Page scripts against a fake data service under Node (TSE chosen over NYSE,
    out-of-window expiry dropped, retry after an empty first snapshot, GET-only
    paths).
  - A read-only live run after the close: 9 of 10 names resolved, 8-32 window
    contracts each; NDA correctly has no CDE options. All quotes were frozen
    after hours and correctly rejected.

## Step 8: Lock Waiting, Clicks After Research, IBKR Quote Filling (October 16)

  - The ~11:21 check left no history row; most likely the browser lock was

- **Lock waiting:**
  - `browser_lock(wait_seconds=...)` waits a bounded time.
  - The 40-minute check waits up to 360 seconds; if still busy it appends a
    `busy` history row.
  - Research waits up to 180 seconds for a running check.
- **Clicks after research:**
  - After each research run's cleanup, while still holding the lock, a ready
    Paper tab gets the same two verified clicks.
  - Logged as `source=session_research`; updates `last_verified_activity_at`.
  - Logged-out Paper never blocks or alters research results.
- **IBKR quote filling.** October 16 Session 2 checked 49 names, but 645
  contracts returned no bid/ask in time (11 qualified). Now:
  - Contracts are quoted best-first (nearest qualifying expiry, nearest
    strike), rotating across names, so a time limit trims the least useful
    contracts, not whole names.
  - Quotes go out in 40-contract waves, with up to eight 1.5-second rounds
    each.
  - Unquoted contracts are counted as `ibkr_quote_not_received` or
    `ibkr_quote_not_requested`, separate from zero bids.
  - A name with no quotes at all falls back to MX.
  - Unknown open interest and volume is labelled `contract_liquidity_unknown`
    and still does not qualify.
